Meet BYOK Chat. The AI chat app that runs on your own API keys.

Chat with your favorite models. Stream answers, reason through documents, and work with files and tools.

Add your own keys for OpenRouter, Anthropic, Gemini, or a local server. They stay in your keychain, and every request goes straight to your provider.

Connect MCP tools, and choose what each chat is allowed to do. Always, ask first, or off.

Keep chats, files, and instructions together in project workspaces.

And always know what a request costs, with usage tracked right on your device.
